A floating pill with an icon and a label — "지도로 보기", "글쓰기", "맨 위로".

```jsx
<ExtendedFloatingActionButton icon={<Icon name="map_regular" size={16} />}>지도 보기</ExtendedFloatingActionButton>
<ExtendedFloatingActionButton variant="overImage" size="small" icon={<Icon name="photo_regular" size={16} />}>사진 12</ExtendedFloatingActionButton>
```

- `overPaper` (gray-900 on white content) is the default; `overImage` is the white pill you place over photography.
