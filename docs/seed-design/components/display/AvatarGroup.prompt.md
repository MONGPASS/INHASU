Overlapping avatars for a group of people — chat participants, 같이 사는 이웃.

```jsx
<AvatarGroup size="small" limit={3} items={members} />
```

- Overlap is a negative gap that tightens with size: -10 (medium), -8 (small), -6 (xsmall), -5 (xxsmall).
- The excess disc is paper-background with a gray-400 hairline and a gray-600 count.
