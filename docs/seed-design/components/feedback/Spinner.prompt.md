Indeterminate loading indicator.

```jsx
<Spinner size="medium" variant="gray" />
<Spinner size="small" variant="white" />
```

- Two sizes only: 24 and 40px. Gray is the default; white over imagery; primary when loading is the main event on screen.
- The arc rotates once per 1200ms with a head/tail sweep — do not swap in a different easing.
