A Callout with a close button — announcements and tips people should be able to dismiss.

```jsx
<DismissableCallout variant="normal" title="새로운 기능" closeIcon={<Icon name="close_regular" size={18} />}>
  이제 동네 인증을 앱에서 바로 할 수 있어요.
</DismissableCallout>
```

- Right padding 14px; the 18px close button sits 10px from the text and inherits the callout's text colour.
